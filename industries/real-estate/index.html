Adaptive Call Intelligence™
Precision-Handling for Every Conversation

Every real estate conversation has its own context. Callers may be frustrated or curious, rushed or relaxed, all business or happy to chat. Knowing how to respond appropriately every time is what makes PATLive’s virtual receptionists shine. They combine deep real estate knowledge with trained judgment to naturally adapt their pace, tone and approach to each conversation – our signature Adaptive Call Intelligence™.

That level of situational awareness and professionalism doesn’t happen by accident. It starts with 140+ hours of real estate–specific Precision Performance Training™ (one of the industry’s most comprehensive programs) and is reinforced every day through ongoing coaching, quality reviews and continuous feedback.

Message Taking

When you can't answer, our real estate trained US–based virtual receptionists step in. Every caller reaches a real person who expertly captures the information you need for organized, confident follow–up.

Lead Collection

You've invested time and money to make the phone ring. We'll make sure someone answers it. PATLive captures new inquiries with a professional first conversation that helps protect your marketing investment and gives every opportunity the attention it deserves.

Client Intake

Every business has its own intake process. Whether you're qualifying investor leads, collecting property information or gathering details for prospective clients, our receptionists follow your customized instructions to capture the information that matters most.

Overflow & Backup

Busy days happen. So do vacations, lunch breaks and unexpected absences. PATLive provides dependable coverage, stepping in when your team is stretched thin so callers still reach a live receptionist, giving you the flexibility of an extended team without the complexity of managing coverage in–house.

After–Hours Coverage

Missing a call can mean missing a huge opportunity. PATLive’s 24/7/365 receptionists will answer calls outside business hours so you can remain responsive – capturing important inquiries and setting the stage for timely follow–up.

Appointment Scheduling

From consultations and property showings to inspections and meetings, PATLive schedules appointments using your availability, booking rules and preferred tools.

Call Screening & Transfers

Not every call deserves the same priority. Our receptionists screen inquiries using your qualification criteria, helping high–value opportunities reach the right person quickly while filtering low–priority or unwanted calls according to your instructions.

Front Desk Receptionist

Need a professional first point of contact without adding another employee? PATLive becomes a seamless extension of your business, delivering a polished caller experience every time the phone rings.

Bilingual Support

Communication shouldn't become a barrier to your next opportunity. Spanish–speaking receptionists help you connect with more callers while delivering the same professional, consistent experience.

How do your virtual receptionists know what to say when answering our calls?

Our receptionists use custom call scripts tailored to your business needs.

Can your service handle after-hours and emergency calls?
Yes, our 24/7 answering service operates around the clock, including after-hours and holidays. We are equipped to handle emergency calls with professionalism and urgency, ensuring your clients receive the support they need at any time.
What integrations do you offer for real estate management software?
